# Deferred material

Live copy of SPEC.md §11.

Preserved so nothing is lost. Not to be developed until the diagnosis is secure.

- **The cure.** Any remedy for the Totalitarian Self.
- **Monism.** The notes' turn to Parmenides and Spinoza: a single all-encompassing substance; "true difference is impossible because pure difference would equal non-existence"; the dismantling of Separation as the root disease of which mind-body dualism is only one symptom.
- **Animalism.** The reclaiming of the body as integral to identity — a useful first step that destroys dualism but supplies no new terms.
- **Heidegger's Being.** Going behind Mind and Consciousness to existence itself.
- **Mortality.** "We think, we feel, and we die"; identity limited to the span of physical existence.
- **The "backward" direction.** The movement forward is behind us — Parmenides and Spinoza as the paths not taken because their monism conflicted with the expanding complexity of self, nation, and market.
