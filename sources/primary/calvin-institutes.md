## Passages

### calvin-institutes.01 · III.xxiv.5 — Christ as the mirror of election

- **Claims:** C4.4 (against, as a claim about Calvin himself)
- **Location:** III.xxiv.5; `texts/calvin-institutes-vol2.txt` around line 9429
- **Checked:** text file

> But if we are chosen in him, we shall find no assurance of our election in ourselves; nor even in God the Father, considered alone, abstractedly from the Son. Christ, therefore, is the mirror, in which it behoves us to contemplate our election; and here we may do it with safety.

Calvin tells believers *not* to look for proof of election in themselves. The practical drive to prove election through work (C4.4) is not found in Calvin's own teaching here. It may still be true of later Calvinism, which is Weber's actual claim. **C4.4 likely needs revising** from "Calvin's predestination drove..." to "Calvinist predestination, as lived by later generations, drove..."
