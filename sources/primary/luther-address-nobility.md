## Passages

### luther-nobility.01 · "The First Wall": the priesthood of all believers

- **Claims:** C4.3 (for), C4.3 (complicates)
- **Location:** "The First Wall," Wace & Buchheim pp. 21–22 (check scan); `texts/luther-first-principles-wace-buchheim.txt` lines 4398–4420
- **Checked:** text file (OCR; spacing cleaned up)

> It has been devised, that the Pope, bishops, priests and monks are called the Spiritual Estate; Princes, lords, artificers and peasants, are the Temporal Estate; which is a very fine, hypocritical device. But let no one be made afraid by it; and that for this reason: That all Christians are truly of the Spiritual Estate, and there is no difference among them, save of office alone. As St. Paul says (1 Cor. xii.), we are all one body, though each member does its own work, to serve the others. [...] Thus we are all consecrated as priests by baptism [...]

**For:** this attacks the social order that assigned spiritual standing by role, which is exactly the "role-assigning order" of C4.1. **Complicates:** the argument is made in the name of *one body* whose members serve one another. The individual is freed from the clerical hierarchy, not from the collective.
