# Sources to pull: the master list

Every copyrighted or hard-to-download source the project still needs, in one place, in the order to pull them. Updated 2026-10-28.

**How to send a source** (easiest first):
1. **Upload the EPUB or PDF** in the chat. Claude cuts out the pages needed.
2. **Send a link** to a full-text copy.
3. **Paste the pages** into the chat: author, title, publisher and year at the top, and `[p. 21]` marks for page breaks.

**Tiers** (from the evidence standard in `planning/claims.md`): **T1** established critics and intellectuals · **T2** data · **T3** institutions · **T4** popular carriers. Claims about the culture need T1 critics plus T2 or T3, so **T1 and T2 come first**.

Chapter titles marked *verify* are from memory: check them against the table of contents.
