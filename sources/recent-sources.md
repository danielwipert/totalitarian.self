# Recent sources (2000–2025)

> **The master list is now `to-pull.md`**, in pull order with tiers. This file keeps the original notes and is no longer updated.

Sources from the last 25 years for working backwards from the present: the period where the Self is claimed to have become a dogma (C1.2, C1.3, C5.14 step 2, C8.6, §7). Compiled 2026-10-25. Older post-1930 works are in `excerpts-needed.md`.

**How to use this list.** Most of these are under copyright. Pull the pages named, save them in `sources/excerpts/` with the edition and `[p. 21]` page marks, and tell Claude (same steps as `excerpts-needed.md`). The works marked **PD** are public domain (US court opinions and federal reports), and Claude can fetch them.

**How to read it.** Each work has a role:
- **Primary:** a carrier of the Self speaking in its own voice. This is the evidence we most need: it shows the Self *as it is lived*, not as critics describe it.
- **Diagnosis:** a critic or scholar who already argues something close to the thesis. Test against these; don't borrow from them. Where they say the same thing, the essay has to show what it adds (C10.1).
- **Data:** surveys and word counts that can show a change happened, and when.
- **Against:** works that dispute the thesis or a piece of it. Read these as hard as the rest.

Publication details were checked by web search on 2026-10-25. Chapter titles marked **verify** are from memory.

Priorities: **1** = decides a claim; **2** = strongly tests a claim; **3** = background.
